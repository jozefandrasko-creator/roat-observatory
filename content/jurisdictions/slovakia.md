Slovakia combines a technically mature EU/UNECE type-approval baseline with a road-operation layer that still relies mainly on the § 49 test-operation permit under Act No. 106/2018 Coll. There is no general legal route from EU type approval to ordinary commercial driverless operation in force. Parliamentary print 1329 would introduce a licensed operator, an approved operation, a control centre and a supervision operator — a genuine second gate — but it is draft law and is coded only as a prospective regime.
