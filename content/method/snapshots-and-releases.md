Coding happens in the ROAT Intelligence Hub, never in this repository. A **snapshot** is a frozen export of one module's coded table at a stated legal snapshot date. Snapshots are immutable: a correction produces a new snapshot that supersedes the old one, and both remain addressable by date.

A snapshot is published only after the companion manuscript has been submitted, as a dated release with a DOI. Until then it may exist in the repository as *draft* or *review* and is shown with a banner.

Every coded cell must cite Hub records at Research Stage *Verified* or above and Evidence Level 1–4. The build refuses a snapshot that cites a *Signal* record or a media, LinkedIn or blog source. The robustness audit published with a snapshot is a same-coder rule-based recoding — a sensitivity check, not inter-coder reliability — until a second human coder exists.
