ROAT is the research group on the regulation of automated transport at the Faculty of Law, Comenius University Bratislava. The Observatory is a research output of the group, not its institutional website; the group itself is presented at flaw.uniba.sk/ROAT.

Work published here is supported by APVV-23-0519 (Legal and Technical Challenges of Smart Mobility to Increase Road Traffic Safety) and related projects catalogued in the Research section.
