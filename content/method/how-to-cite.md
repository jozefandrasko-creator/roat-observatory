Cite a snapshot, not a page: the snapshot identifier and date fix exactly which coded table you relied on.

> ROAT Observatory, *Regulatory normalisation by functional layer* [ROAT-MOD-LAYERS], snapshot 2026-08-25 [ROAT-SNAP-LAYERS-2026-08-25], DOI (when minted). Faculty of Law, Comenius University Bratislava.

Every ROAT identifier resolves at `/id/<ROAT-ID>`. Hub record identifiers (`ROAT-2026-NNNN`) resolve to the Source Library once the record is published there. Content is licensed CC BY 4.0; the repository tooling is MIT.
