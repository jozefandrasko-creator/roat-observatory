The failure mode this module exists to prevent is collapsing distinct functions into one generic "operator". Each row is one role as a framework defines it; each column is one function it may or may not perform. Remote assistance, remote intervention and remote driving are kept apart because the legally decisive question is whether the automated driving system continues to perform the dynamic driving task or a human takes over part of it.

This module is published as a draft snapshot until its companion article has been submitted.
